Document sharing roadmap

Global `documents:read` is sufficient for the early local platform but not for a
shared enterprise knowledge base. The next authorization phase introduces an
explicit document-access policy and durable access grants.

Retrieval and RAG must enforce the same rule. Qdrant can propose similar chunks,
but PostgreSQL will decide whether the requesting user may receive each document
and whether a citation may name it. Revoking a grant must affect later searches
and chat turns without waiting for vectors to be rebuilt.

Tenant isolation, organization roles, and broad enterprise governance follow in
later phases after document-level behavior is proven and tested.
