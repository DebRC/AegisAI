Business continuity notes

The service depends on PostgreSQL for authoritative metadata and access state,
Redis for background-task transport, Qdrant for derived vectors, and document
storage for original bytes. A temporary Qdrant outage prevents semantic search
but must not cause PostgreSQL records to be treated as invalid.

Restore in order: database integrity and migrations, original document storage,
worker connectivity, then vector-index health. Rebuild derived vectors through
the controlled indexing workflow when necessary; never fabricate embeddings or
mark indexing succeeded without traceable records.

Exercise recovery procedures regularly and record evidence of the restore test.
