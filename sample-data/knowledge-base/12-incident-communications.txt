Incident communications

The incident commander owns external status updates. State the observed impact,
the current mitigation, the next update time, and what is still unknown. Avoid
guessing at root cause before evidence is reviewed.

For a Severity 1 event, post an initial stakeholder update within fifteen
minutes and update at least every thirty minutes until the service is stable.
For Severity 2, provide an initial update within thirty minutes and a revised
cadence suitable for the impact.

After recovery, preserve logs and the timeline, then schedule a blameless review
with corrective actions and named owners.
