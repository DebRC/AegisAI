Incident severity guide

Severity 1 means confirmed loss of confidentiality, integrity, or availability
that materially affects many users or a critical service. Page the incident
commander immediately, create a dedicated incident channel, and start a
time-stamped timeline.

Severity 2 means a significant degradation with a workaround or limited blast
radius. Assign an incident lead, communicate an initial assessment within thirty
minutes, and reassess severity as evidence changes.

Severity 3 means a contained defect or operational issue with no material user
impact. Record the issue, assign an owner, and schedule normal remediation.
