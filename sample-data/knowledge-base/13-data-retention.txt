Data retention standard

Keep active customer knowledge documents only for the approved business purpose.
When the purpose ends, delete the document through the application lifecycle so
metadata is hidden, background work is cancelled, and derived vectors can be
cleaned up.

Application audit records are retained for twelve months unless a legal hold or
approved exception applies. Security incident evidence is retained until the
incident review and all corrective actions are complete.

Do not use a retention rule to preserve authentication tokens, provider secrets,
or raw passwords. Those values must never be stored in ordinary documents.
