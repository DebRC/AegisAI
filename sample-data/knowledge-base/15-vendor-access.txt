Third-party vendor access

Give vendors the least access needed for a time-bound support task. A vendor
account must be named, individually attributable, and approved by the service
owner. Shared administrative accounts are prohibited.

Before granting access, confirm the business purpose, scope, expiry, and audit
trail. Revoke access at the end of the task and immediately if the vendor
relationship changes or suspicious activity is observed.

Vendor access does not replace AegisAI local authorization. External identity
claims alone never grant a local role or `documents:read` permission.
